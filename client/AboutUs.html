About Stocks Now

Stocks Now is a student-developed platform designed to help users understand stock market trends and explore investment information in a simple and easy way.

Our Purpose

Understanding the stock market can sometimes be difficult for beginners. Stocks Now was created to make market information easier to explore and understand through a simple and user-friendly platform.

What We Offer

📊 Market Overview

Explore market trends and understand the performance of different markets.

📈 Stock Insights

Get useful information about stocks and market performance.

💼 Portfolio Building

Learn about diversification and explore ways to build a better portfolio.

⚡ Simple & Fast

Access market information through a clean and easy-to-use interface.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make stock market information simple and accessible for students, beginners, and anyone interested in learning more about financial markets.

Developed By

Stocks Now is developed as a student project by students of BML Munjal University.

⚠️ Disclaimer

Stocks Now is an educational project. The information provided on this website is for learning and informational purposes only and should not be considered professional financial or investment advice.
