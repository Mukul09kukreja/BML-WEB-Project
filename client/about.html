About the project

Learn the principles. Build your confidence.

StockPie is a student-built stock market simulator for supported NSE stocks, with prices from Yahoo Finance.

Why we built it

We built a simple place to practice buying and selling NSE stocks using virtual cash and timestamped market quotes.

StockPie stores account credentials as password hashes and keeps each user's purchase records in MongoDB.

How it works

Three steps, one habit.

01

Choose an NSE stock

Choose from the supported NSE stock list and check the current price before saving a purchase.

02

Place a virtual trade

Buy within your virtual cash balance, or sell shares you own. Every account starts with ₹10 lakh in virtual cash.

03

Track your progress

Your portfolio combines purchases by stock and compares the saved buy price with the latest available quote.

Risk disclosure

What this site is not.

StockPie is an academic project. It is not a broker, does not hold client money and does not execute real orders.

Trades are simulated only. Current prices come from Yahoo Finance through yfinance. Nothing here is investment advice.

Investing in real markets involves risk, including the possible loss of the money you invest. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
