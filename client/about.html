About the project

Passive investing, without the risk.

StockPie is a student-built market simulator. Explore sample fund allocations and portfolio performance without using real money. Simulated order execution is planned for the next phase.

Why we built it

Most first-time investors learn about markets through losses. We wanted a place to learn the other way around: understand diversification, see the effect of fees, watch a portfolio drift away from its target allocation — and fix it — before any real money is involved.

The product is deliberately centred on passive funds. Instead of picking stocks to beat the index, you choose a strategy, allocate across asset classes and then monitor how the portfolio behaves.

How it works

Three steps, one habit.

01

Pick a strategy

Conservative, Balanced or Growth. Each one defines a target mix of equities, bonds and gold before you buy anything.

02

Allocate with funds

Buy broad index funds rather than single companies. Your portfolio value comes from the funds you hold, not from timing the market.

03

Track and rebalance

Watch profit and loss, notice drift from your target weights, and rebalance back on purpose instead of on impulse.

Risk disclosure

What this site is not.

StockPie is an academic project. It is not a broker, it does not hold client money and it does not execute real orders.

All prices, indices, news items and portfolio figures on this site are simulated demonstration data. Nothing here is investment advice, and no projection shown by the calculator is a promise of future returns.

Investing in real markets involves risk, including the possible loss of the money you invest. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
