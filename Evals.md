## The most important overall requirements

**Your project should:**

> **Start simple → become interactive → become full-stack.**

And throughout the project:

* Everyone must understand **HTML, CSS, JS, and backend integration**.
* Roles should **rotate**, not be permanently divided by technology.
* Your work must be on a **public GitHub repository**.
* You should be able to **explain and modify your own code during viva/live coding**.
* The project should have enough scope for **innovation**, but remain realistically achievable.
* **Understanding matters more than complexity** because AI-generated code/plagiarism can lead to UFM action.

### In one line:

**A1 = Design & Build → A2 = Interact → A3 = Integrate & Deliver.**
